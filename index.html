The Polling Booth Experience 🚶‍♂️

What actually happens when you step inside? It's a quick, secure 4-step process:

  • Step 1: Identity Check - Officials check your name in the voter list and verify your ID.
  • Step 2: The Ink - A polling officer applies indelible ink on your left forefinger – the mark of a voter!
  • Step 3: The Register - You sign the register (Form 17A) and get a small slip.
  • Step 4: The Vote - You enter the secret compartment, press the button on the EVM, and verify your choice on the VVPAT screen.

The Tech Behind the Vote 🗳️

India uses a 3-unit system to keep your vote secure and verifiable.

🔘

Balloting Unit (BU)

The "keyboard" of the election. You press the blue button next to your candidate's name and symbol.

📄

VVPAT

The "Receipt". It shows a paper slip with your candidate's details for 7 seconds before it drops into a sealed box. No more doubts!

📟

Control Unit (CU)

The "Brain". Kept with the Polling Officer, it records the vote only after the officer enables it. One person, one vote!
